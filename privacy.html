Privacy Policy

Statement Fetcher is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • It requests read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly) solely to find and download the owner's own bank statement PDF attachments.
  • Downloaded files and OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's local machine.
  • No data is collected from other people, and no data is shared, sold, or transferred to any third party.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
